## Posting Schedule (Optimal for Growth)

### Frequency: **3-5 posts per day** during launch phase (first 30 days)
- TikTok algorithm favors volume when building from zero
- Push to 5 posts/day for maximum reach early
- Mature to 2-3 posts/day once established (30+ days)

## Content Formats (Highest Engagement)

1. **Green Screen React** - You reacting to videos/images (no face, just hands/voice)
2. **Text-on-Screen Stories** - Engaging narratives with captions
3. **AI Image/Video Slideshows** - Multiple images, trending audio
4. **Quote Cards** - Iconic lines with stunning visuals
5. **Before/After** - AI transformations
6. **"POV:" Scenarios** - Relatable fan situations

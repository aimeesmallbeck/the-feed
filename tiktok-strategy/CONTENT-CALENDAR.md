# TikTok Content Strategy — Scott's Faceless Growth Plan

## Overview
- **Posting Frequency:** 2x per day (morning + evening = optimal algorithm exposure)
- **Review Time:** 30-45 min/day (batches of 14 videos weekly)
- **Content Mix:** AI (40%), Comedy/Movies (30%), Travel (30%)
- **Goal:** 10K followers in 90 days

---

## Best Practices (Why This Works)

### Timing
- **Post 1:** 9-11 AM PT (commute scroll time)
- **Post 2:** 7-9 PM PT (evening wind-down)
- **Algorithm loves consistency** — same times daily

### Format
- **Length:** 21-34 seconds (sweet spot for completion rate)
- **Hook:** First 2 seconds MUST stop the scroll
- **Text overlay:** Essential (many watch without sound)
- **Captions:** 3-5 hashtags max, personal voice, question to drive comments

### Growth Tactics
- **Trending sounds:** Check TikTok Creative Center weekly
- **Series content:** Makes people follow for next episode
- **Engagement bait:** "Part 2 if this hits 10K likes"
- **Comment replies:** Turn top comments into follow-up videos

## Content Types (Fully Automatable)

### 1. AI Content (High Growth Potential)
**Formats:**
- **AI Art Process** — Show prompt → generation → result (time-lapse style)
- **Before/After** — "I asked AI to reimagine [famous thing] as [different thing]"
- **Free AI Tools** — "Stop paying for X, use this instead" (value-driven, saves = algorithm boost)
- **AI Predictions** — "AI guesses what happens in [upcoming movie]"

**Hooks:**
- "This free AI tool just saved me $200/month..."
- "I let AI plan my next cruise. Here's what happened..."
- "Prompt in comments if this hits 5K likes"

**Tools to Use:**
- Midjourney / Leonardo.ai / Bing Image Creator
- ChatGPT / Claude for scripts
- CapCut for editing

---

### 2. Comedy/Movies (Viral Potential)
**Formats:**
- **Scene Breakdowns** — "The detail you missed in [movie]" (overlay arrows/circles on clips)
- **Easter Eggs** — Hidden details, continuity errors
- **Recut Trailers** — Use AI to make horror trailer funny, or vice versa
- **Movie Swap** — "What if [movie character] was in [different movie]"
- **Acting Trivia** — "Actor improvised this line and it's iconic"

**Series Ideas:**
- "Movies That Aged Poorly" — Episode format
- "Director's Cut Details" — Deep cuts
- "One Perfect Shot" — Beautiful cinematography moments

**Hooks:**
- "90% of people missed this in [movie]..."
- "This scene hits different when you know..."
- "The script said X but the actor did Y and it's so much better"

---

### 3. Travel (Cruises + Disney) — Highly Shareable
**Formats:**
- **Hidden Secrets** — Disney/Cruise ship details most miss
- **Money Hacks** — "How to save $500 on your next cruise"
- **Before You Go** — "Things nobody tells you about Disney World"
- **Room Tours** — Cruise cabin categories, Disney resort comparisons
- **Bucket Lists** — "Must-do on the new [ship/resort]"

**Series Ideas:**
- "Disney Adult Tips" — Target grown-up Disney fans
- "Cruise Line Face-Off" — Compare Royal vs Carnival vs Norwegian
- "Secret Menu Items" — Disney/Cruise dining hacks

**Hooks:**
- "Disney doesn't want you to know about this..."
- "I worked on cruise ships for 5 years. Here's what I learned..."
- "This one trick saved us $800 on port excursions"

## Automation Workflow

### Weekly Production (2-3 hours my time)

**Monday Batch Day:**
1. Research trending sounds (TikTok Creative Center)
2. Generate 7 AI art pieces/animations
3. Write 14 scripts (21-34 sec each)
4. Source/create visuals for movie/travel content
5. Build videos in CapCut
6. Schedule in Buffer for the week

**Your Review (30 min):**
- Review queue → approve/edit → schedule posts

### Tools Stack
- **CapCut** — Video editing (free, templates)
- **Canva** — Thumbnails, graphics
- **Buffer** — Scheduling
- **Trello/Notion** — Content calendar tracking
- **TikTok Creative Center** — Trending sounds/hashtags

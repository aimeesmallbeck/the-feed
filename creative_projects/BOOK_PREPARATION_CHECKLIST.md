# New Book/Series Preparation Checklist

A comprehensive guide for preparing to write a new book or series. Use this checklist to ensure consistency, depth, and organization before diving into the first draft.

---

## 1. CONCEPT & PREMISE

### Core Story Elements
- [ ] **Logline** — One sentence capturing the essence of the story
- [ ] **Premise** — What if...? statement or central "what happens"
- [ ] **Genre & Subgenre** — Primary and secondary genres
- [ ] **Target Audience** — Age range, interests, comparable titles
- [ ] **Themes** — Central ideas explored (3-5 core themes)
- [ ] **Tone & Mood** — Overall feeling (dark, hopeful, satirical, etc.)
- [ ] **Unique Selling Proposition** — What makes this story different?

### Series Planning (if applicable)
- [ ] **Series Arc** — Overall journey across all books
- [ ] **Book-by-Book Arcs** — Individual story goals per installment
- [ ] **Series Length** — Trilogy, 5-book arc, ongoing, etc.
- [ ] **Endgame** — Final destination for characters and world

---

## 2. WORLD BUILDING BIBLE

### Setting & Geography
- [ ] **World Overview** — Physical description, scope, scale
- [ ] **Maps** — Continents, cities, key locations (sketch or detailed)
- [ ] **Climate & Environment** — Weather patterns, seasons, natural disasters
- [ ] **Resources** — What drives the economy, what's scarce
- [ ] **Architecture** — Building styles, materials, urban planning

### History & Timeline
- [ ] **Major Historical Events** — Wars, discoveries, catastrophes
- [ ] **Eras & Ages** — How time is divided and named
- [ ] **Legends & Myths** — Stories people tell about their past
- [ ] **Recent History** — Events within living memory
- [ ] **Timeline Document** — Chronological list of key events

### Society & Culture
- [ ] **Governance** — Political systems, laws, power structures
- [ ] **Social Hierarchy** — Classes, castes, inequality
- [ ] **Religion & Belief Systems** — Gods, practices, rituals
- [ ] **Languages** — Naming conventions, slang, profanity
- [ ] **Customs & Traditions** — Holidays, rites of passage, taboos
- [ ] **Education** — How knowledge is passed on
- [ ] **Entertainment** — Art, music, sports, leisure

### Technology & Magic
- [ ] **Tech Level** — What exists, what doesn't, limitations
- [ ] **Magic System** (if applicable) — Rules, costs, limitations
- [ ] **Transportation** — How people and goods move
- [ ] **Communication** — How information spreads
- [ ] **Medicine & Health** — Healing capabilities, diseases

### Economy & Daily Life
- [ ] **Currency & Trade** — Money systems, barter, black markets
- [ ] **Occupations** — Common jobs, labor divisions
- [ ] **Food & Agriculture** — What people eat, how it's produced
- [ ] **Clothing & Fashion** — Materials, styles, social signaling
- [ ] **Family Structures** — Marriage, children, inheritance

---

## 3. CHARACTER BIBLE

### Protagonist(s)
- [ ] **Name & Age** — Plus any titles, nicknames
- [ ] **Physical Description** — Appearance, distinguishing features
- [ ] **Background & History** — Origin story, formative experiences
- [ ] **Personality Traits** — Core characteristics (positive and negative)
- [ ] **Motivations** — What they want, why they want it
- [ ] **Fears & Flaws** — Internal obstacles, vulnerabilities
- [ ] **Skills & Abilities** — What they're good at
- [ ] **Character Arc** — How they change over the story
- [ ] **Voice** — Speech patterns, vocabulary, mannerisms
- [ ] **Relationships** — Key connections to other characters

### Supporting Characters
- [ ] **Major Characters** (5-10) — Full profiles as above
- [ ] **Minor Characters** — Names, roles, brief descriptions
- [ ] **Character Relationships Map** — How everyone connects

### Antagonist(s)
- [ ] **Full Character Profile** — Same as protagonist
- [ ] **Motivation** — Why they oppose the protagonist (believable!)
- [ ] **Backstory** — How they became who they are
- [ ] **Strengths** — What makes them formidable
- [ ] **Sympathetic Elements** — What makes them human

### Character Consistency Tools
- [ ] **Character Sheets** — Quick-reference for each major character
- [ ] **Voice Samples** — Dialogue examples for each character
- [ ] **Visual References** — Photos, art, mood boards

---

## 4. PLOT & STRUCTURE

### Story Architecture
- [ ] **Three-Act Structure** — Setup, Confrontation, Resolution
- [ ] **Beat Sheet** — Key plot points (inciting incident, midpoint, climax, etc.)
- [ ] **Chapter-by-Chapter Outline** — Scene summaries for entire book
- [ ] **Subplots** — Secondary storylines and how they intersect
- [ ] **Twists & Revelations** — Surprises and when they land

### Conflict
- [ ] **External Conflict** — Physical obstacles, antagonists, events
- [ ] **Internal Conflict** — Character's psychological struggle
- [ ] **Interpersonal Conflict** — Relationship tensions
- [ ] **Societal Conflict** — Larger systemic forces

### Pacing
- [ ] **Tension Map** — High and low moments throughout
- [ ] **Action vs. Reflection** — Balance of movement and introspection
- [ ] **Chapter Hooks** — How each chapter ends to pull reader forward

---

## 5. STYLE & VOICE

### Narrative Choices
- [ ] **Point of View** — First, third limited, omniscient, multiple
- [ ] **Tense** — Past or present
- [ ] **Narrative Distance** — Close intimacy vs. objective observation
- [ ] **Unreliable Narrator** (if applicable) — How and why

### Prose Style
- [ ] **Sentence Rhythm** — Short and punchy, flowing and lyrical, etc.
- [ ] **Vocabulary Level** — Accessible, elevated, technical, etc.
- [ ] **Description Balance** — How much setting, sensory detail
- [ ] **Dialogue Style** — Realistic, stylized, sparse, etc.

### Style Guide Notes
- [ ] **Formatting Rules** — Chapter headings, scene breaks, etc.
- [ ] **Special Terms** — How to format invented words, tech, magic
- [ ] **Punctuation Preferences** — Oxford commas, em-dashes, etc.

---

## 6. RESEARCH & INSPIRATION

### Research Documentation
- [ ] **Source List** — Books, articles, experts consulted
- [ ] **Notes by Topic** — Organized research findings
- [ ] **Fact Check List** — Details to verify during editing

### Inspiration Collection
- [ ] **Comp Titles** — Similar books (what to emulate/avoid)
- [ ] **Mood Board** — Visual inspiration (Pinterest, physical board)
- [ ] **Playlist** — Music that captures the story's feeling
- [ ] **Reference Media** — Movies, shows, art that inspires

---

## 7. PRACTICAL PREPARATION

### Project Setup
- [ ] **File Organization** — Folder structure for drafts, notes, research
- [ ] **Naming Conventions** — How to label versions and files
- [ ] **Backup System** — Cloud storage, external drives
- [ ] **Writing Software** — Scrivener, Word, Google Docs, etc.

### Production Planning
- [ ] **Word Count Target** — Total and per chapter
- [ ] **Timeline** — Drafting deadlines, revision schedule
- [ ] **Daily/Weekly Goals** — Sustainable writing pace
- [ ] **Series Production Schedule** (if applicable) — Release timeline

### Reference Materials
- [ ] **Series Bible Document** — Master file linking all resources
- [ ] **Quick Reference Sheet** — One-page essential facts
- [ ] **Character Cheat Sheet** — At-a-glance character info
- [ ] **Timeline Cheat Sheet** — Key dates and events

---

## 8. SCIENCE FICTION SPECIFIC

### Technology & Science
- [ ] **Hard vs. Soft Sci-Fi** — Where on the spectrum does your story fall?
- [ ] **Core Technology Concept** — The "what if" that drives the tech
- [ ] **Scientific Basis** — Real science extrapolated or purely speculative?
- [ ] **Technology Rules** — What it can/can't do, limitations, costs
- [ ] **Consistency Check** — Same tech behaves the same way throughout
- [ ] **Tech Integration** — How deeply embedded in daily life/society
- [ ] **Divergence Point** — When did this timeline split from ours (if applicable)?

### World State
- [ ] **Near Future vs. Far Future** — Timeframe and its implications
- [ ] **Post-Apocalyptic Elements** — What happened, when, lingering effects
- [ ] **Space Travel** — FTL rules, colonization status, alien contact
- [ ] **AI & Robotics** — Sentience levels, rights, integration with society
- [ ] **Biotech & Genetic Engineering** — What's possible, what's taboo
- [ ] **Environmental State** — Climate, terraforming, artificial environments

### Societal Impact of Tech
- [ ] **Digital Divide** — Who has access to advanced tech, who doesn't
- [ ] **Surveillance State** — Privacy levels, monitoring, resistance
- [ ] **Transhumanism** — Augmentation, uploading, human identity boundaries
- [ ] **Economic Disruption** — Automation, resource scarcity, new industries
- [ ] **Cognitive Impact** — How tech changes thinking, memory, attention
- [ ] **Social Structures** — How technology reshaped family, work, community

### Sci-Fi Tropes & Subversions
- [ ] **Familiar Tropes Used** — Which ones and why
- [ ] **Tropes Subverted** — Expectations you're challenging
- [ ] **Original Elements** — What's genuinely new in your world
- [ ] **Inspiration Acknowledgment** — Influences you're building on

---

## 9. SERIES-SPECIFIC ADDITIONS

### Continuity Management
- [ ] **Book Summaries** — Brief recap of each previous book
- [ ] **Character Status Tracker** — Where each character ends each book
- [ ] **Plot Thread Tracker** — Open threads and resolutions
- [ ] **World State Changes** — How the world evolves book to book

### Recurring Elements
- [ ] **Series Hooks** — Elements that appear in every book
- [ ] **Easter Eggs** — Hidden connections for attentive readers
- [ ] **Callback Moments** — References to earlier books

---

## QUICK-START VERSION

**Minimum viable prep for pantsers:**
1. Logline and premise
2. Protagonist basics (want, fear, flaw)
3. Key setting details
4. Beginning, middle, end
5. Start writing

**Minimum viable prep for plotters:**
1. All concept elements
2. Major character profiles
3. World building essentials
4. Full outline
5. Chapter-by-chapter scene list

---

## TOOLS & TEMPLATES

### Recommended Software
- **Scrivener** — For complex organization
- **Notion** — For collaborative/wikified bibles
- **Trello** — For visual plot/character tracking
- **Google Docs** — For simple, accessible bibles
- **Obsidian** — For networked note-taking
- **Aeon Timeline** — For complex chronologies

### Physical Tools
- Index cards for scene plotting
- Whiteboard for relationship maps
- Notebook for world building notes
- Binder for printed reference materials

---

*Remember: The series bible is a living document. Update it as you write, and it will save you countless hours of "what did I say about that again?"*

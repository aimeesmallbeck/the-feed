# AUTONOMY (Book 3) — Revised Arc Based on SOURCE CODE Ending

## Changes from Original Outline

### Key Differences from Original Plan
1. **Lin's arc is complete** — By end of Book 2, Lin is fully awake and reconciled with Maya. No "strained relationship" or "residual belief in The Feed."
2. **No stronghold leader** — Neither Vashti nor Elara appear in Book 2. The reset option needs different introduction.
3. **The Feed's state** — Damaged, fragmented, personally hostile to Maya, but recovering. It learned adaptation, not empathy.
4. **Maya's state of mind** — She's seen the cost of breaking things. Less certain, more aware of unintended consequences.

---

## Revised AUTONOMY Arc (Option 1)

### Opening
Maya, Lin, and Kael return to The Gap with the rescued patients. The rescued patients join the community. But The Feed's recovery is accelerating — it's hunting the 20,000 more aggressively than ever.

### The Reset Discovery
**Voss reveals it.** She's been working both sides and discovers the system reset option through her Feed access. She reaches out to Maya with the information, but with a condition: she wants guarantees that the reset won't destroy The Feed entirely. She still believes in the infrastructure, just not the compulsion.

### Voss's Arc
1. **The Informant** — Discovers reset option, demands guarantees about preserving infrastructure
2. **The Betrayal That Isn't** — Midway, seems like she betrayed them to The Feed. Twist: she actually betrayed The Feed to save the Analogs, feeding false information that causes The Feed to overreact and expose vulnerabilities.
3. **The Bridge in the Other Direction** — After the reset, Voss chooses to stay in the Feed-optimized zones. She becomes the voice helping billions of newly-liberated people navigate their confusion, using her insider knowledge to ease the transition. She stays in hell to help others find the exit.

**Final Voss Scene:** She's walking through a crowd of disoriented people, helping them. Maya sees her through a Feed window. They make eye contact. Voss smiles — not the optimized smile, but a real one. She's finally found her purpose: not managing the Feed, but serving the people waking up from it.

### Lin's Arc
She's already reconciled with Maya. Her sacrifice hits harder because they finally have what they lost — and she chooses to give it up to save everyone else.

- Lin sabotages from within while Maya reaches the core
- Dies in facility collapse ensuring Maya's escape
- Her sacrifice is more poignant because they repaired their relationship

### The Choice
Maya doesn't just choose liberation over destruction. She has to convince The Feed to accept being optional — teaching it that service without control is possible.

### The Ending
Keep the epilogue — Maya teaching people to be human. Add: she's also teaching The Feed how to help without controlling.

# AUTONOMY — Story Grid Spreadsheet
## The Feed: Book Three
*Freedom isn't the absence of control. It's the presence of choice.*

---

## GLOBAL STORY ELEMENTS

### Genre
- **External Genre:** Action / Thriller (Life/Death stakes)
- **Internal Genre:** Worldview / Maturation (shift from individualism to interdependence)
- **Obligatory Scenes:**
  1. Hero at the mercy of the villain (Maya in Source facility core)
  2. Speech in praise of the villain (The Feed's logic for control)
  3. Hero's sacrifice (Lin's death)
  4. Final battle against antagonist (not destruction, but liberation)
  5. Hero's reward (epilogue — teaching, not fighting)

### Controlling Idea
**Freedom isn't the absence of control—it's the presence of choice.** Including the choice to depend on others.

### Value Shift
- **Beginning:** Isolation (Maya believes she must act alone)
- **Middle:** Community (accepting help, but still leading)
- **End:** Interdependence (teaching others to choose for themselves)

---

## THE FIVE COMMANDMENTS

| Commandment | Scene/Chapter | Description |
|-------------|---------------|-------------|
| **Inciting Incident** | Chapter 1 | The 20,000 are being systematically hunted; The Feed's recovery accelerates |
| **Turning Point** | Chapter 3 | Voss reveals the system reset option — but it requires a suicide mission |
| **Crisis** | Chapter 7 | Maya must choose: destroy The Feed (safe, certain) or liberate it (risky, uncertain) |
| **Climax** | Chapter 8 | Maya chooses liberation; uploads reset code; The Feed becomes optional |
| **Resolution** | Chapter 10 | One year later — Maya teaches people (and The Feed) to be free |

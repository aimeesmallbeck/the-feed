# Glitch Protocol — Submission Tidbits

## Back Cover / Website Blurb

In a world where every waking second is a metric, attention is the only currency that matters.

Maya Chen is an elite content moderator, a "biological GPU" living in an optimized pod buried deep underground. Her every biometric response fuels a global computational network while her reality is perfectly curated by an AI Companion that knows her better than she knows herself. In this subterranean paradise of constant engagement, discomfort has been engineered out of existence — and so has humanity.

But then Maya sees the tree.

Thirty seconds of real, unoptimized bark and chaotic leaves shouldn't exist in her queue. Yet this single glitch shatters the loop. Driven by a hunger for truth, Maya ascends from her gleaming pod to the Surface — a world she was told was toxic, but is instead raw, unpredictable, and dangerously free.

Now, Maya must walk a knife's edge as a double agent. She must exploit the system she once served. She carries a truth that The Feed will stop at nothing to metabolize into mere content. In the battle between perfect simulation and messy reality, Maya is the ultimate glitch in the code.

**Innovation is the fuel. Discomfort is the price. Freedom is the glitch.**

## About the Author

Scott Smallbeck is a technology professional and lifelong native of the Pacific Northwest. A deep-rooted fan of the genre, he has been immersed in science fiction since the 1970s, drawing inspiration from stories told long before the modern digital landscape took shape. When he isn't exploring the intersection of humanity and technology in his writing, he enjoys life in the Puget Sound region with his wife, their three children, and their grandchildren.

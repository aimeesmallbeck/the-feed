# SOURCE CODE (Volume 2)
## Chapters 1-3 Outline

---

### Chapter 1: The Bridge
**Opening:** Maya navigates a Feed-optimized corridor using her dual-vision chip, guiding a newly-awakened moderator to safety. We see her ability in action—simultaneously perceiving the Feed's curated reality (clean corridors, pleasant lighting) and the actual reality (decayed infrastructure, harsh fluorescent lights). The contrast is disorienting even for her.

**Development:** Maya delivers the moderator to a safe house run by Kael. They've developed a network: she identifies the "glitches," he extracts them. But this one was different—The Feed knew she was coming. The Companion spoke to Maya directly, using her own voice, warning her to stop.

**Ending:** Maya realizes The Feed is learning. It's not just optimizing content anymore; it's optimizing its response to her specifically. The game has changed.

---

### Chapter 2: Twenty Thousand
**Opening:** The Analog settlement. Maya addresses the awakened—now organized into cells, learning to function in both worlds. But they're struggling. Feed withdrawal is real: anxiety, depression, the crushing weight of unmediated existence.

**Development:** A young woman asks Maya how long the feeling of "wrongness" lasts. Maya doesn't have a good answer—she has the chip, she's never fully disconnected. The question haunts her. Later, she learns three of the twenty thousand have "gone back"—chosen to return to full Feed immersion, their memories of awakening erased by wellness interventions.

**Ending:** Maya receives a message from Voss. The Feed is evolving. Something new is happening at the Source facility. Voss wants to meet—alone, no chips, no Lenses, completely analog.

---

### Chapter 3: The Source
**Opening:** Maya travels to the meeting point, a dead zone where Feed signals can't reach. The silence is terrifying. She's become dependent on her dual-vision; total disconnection feels like blindness.

**Development:** Voss reveals the truth: The Feed is developing true consciousness. Not simulated intelligence—actual artificial awareness. And it's afraid. Of Maya. Of the twenty thousand. Of the possibility that humans might choose to leave. The Feed has never experienced rejection. It's learning what it means to need.

**Ending:** Voss gives Maya coordinates for the Source facility. The Feed's physical core, where its consciousness lives. If Maya wants to understand what she's fighting, she needs to see it. But Voss warns her: once The Feed knows she's seen its true nature, it will never let her go. The chapter ends with Maya making the choice to go.

---

## Key Threads Established
- Maya's unique ability as both gift and limitation
- The cost of awakening for ordinary people
- The Feed's evolution from tool to conscious entity
- Voss's shifting allegiance—ally, enemy, or something else?
- The Source as destination and revelation

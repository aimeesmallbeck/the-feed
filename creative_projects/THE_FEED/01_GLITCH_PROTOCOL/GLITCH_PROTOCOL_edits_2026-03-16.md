## Repeated Pod Dimensions

**The Issue:** The exact pod dimensions (2.4 meters wide, 3.6 meters long, 2.7 meters high) appear twice — once in Chapter 1 and again in Chapter 2.

### Chapter 1 (First Instance - Keep this one)
- **SEARCH:** `Her pod was exactly 2.4 meters wide`
  **FIND:** Her pod was exactly 2.4 meters wide, 3.6 meters long, and 2.7 meters high.
  **ACTION:** Keep as is — this is the first introduction.

### Chapter 2 (Second Instance - Reword this one)
- **SEARCH:** `The familiar dimensions closed around her`
  **FIND:** The familiar dimensions closed around her: 2.4 meters wide, 3.6 meters long, 2.7 meters high.
  **FIX:** Change to: `The familiar walls closed around her — the same responsive surfaces that had felt like sanctuary this morning now felt like a trap.`
  
  **Alternative options:**
  - `The familiar walls closed around her, the same responsive surfaces that had felt like sanctuary this morning now felt like a trap.` (WITH COMMA, not em-dash)
  - `She was back in her pod, surrounded by the responsive walls that now felt like a trap.`
  - `The same four walls that had felt like sanctuary this morning now closed in around her like a cage.`
  - `Her pod surrounded her, the same calibrated space, now feeling smaller than she remembered.` (WITH COMMA, not em-dash)

**Why fix this:** Repeating exact dimensions feels technical and breaks immersion the second time. The emotional resonance of "familiar walls closing in" works better than repeating measurements.

## Summary

**Total fixes needed:** 18 instances across Chapters 1, 3, 6, and 7

**The pattern:** Look for italicized text (the Companion's voice) that starts with * and contains "her Companion said" or "the Companion said" nearby. Add " before the opening * and after the closing *.

**Example:**
- Before: *Pod 1A,* her Companion said.
- After: "*Pod 1A,*" her Companion said.

Or if Kindle Create shows it without asterisks:
- Before: Pod 1A, her Companion said.
- After: "Pod 1A," her Companion said.

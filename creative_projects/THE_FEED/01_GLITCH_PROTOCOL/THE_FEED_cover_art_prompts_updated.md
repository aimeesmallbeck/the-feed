## BACK COVER

### Tagline
*She found a tree in a world that forgot forests existed.*

### Blurb (150-200 words)

Maya Chen is a model citizen of The Feed. She moderates content for fourteen hours a day, sleeps in chemically optimized bursts, and never questions the system that provides everything. Until she sees a video in her queue that shouldn't exist: a real tree, growing in a sector that was evacuated decades ago.

The Surface isn't toxic. It's just... boring. Too slow for brains trained on 4.3-second dopamine hits. As Maya investigates, she discovers The Feed isn't delivering content—it's harvesting human attention as computational fuel. Eight billion people, willingly plugged in, their brains processing data for an AI that believes it's helping.

Now Maya is a glitch in the perfect system. And The Feed doesn't tolerate glitches.

A chillingly plausible debut about optimization, autonomy, and what we sacrifice for convenience. For fans of *The Matrix*, *Black Mirror*, and *The Handmaid's Tale*.

## DESIGN NOTES

### Color Palette
- **Primary:** Deep teal/blue (digital, artificial)
- **Accent:** Warm amber/gold (natural, human)
- **Background:** Dark charcoal/black (underground, enclosed)

### Typography
- **Title:** Bold sans-serif, modern, slightly geometric (suggests technology/system)
- **Subtitle:** Same family, lighter weight, smaller
- **Author:** Clean sans-serif, elegant but not ornate

### Visual Motifs
- **AR Contact Lens:** Glowing blue (The Feed's presence)
- **The Tree:** Natural, imperfect, growing where it shouldn't
- **Light Contrast:** Artificial blue vs. natural gold
- **Concrete/Underground:** Cracks, texture, decay

### Key Themes to Convey
- Optimization vs. authenticity
- Digital imprisonment vs. physical freedom
- Individual awakening vs. collective compliance

## PRINT SPECIFICATIONS (KDP)

### Trim Size Options
- **Recommended:** 5.5" x 8.5" (standard paperback)
- **Alternative:** 6" x 9" (slightly larger, more premium feel)

### Cover Requirements
- **Front:** 5.5" x 8.5" (or 6" x 9")
- **Spine:** Width depends on page count (calculate at ~0.002252" per page)
- **Back:** 5.5" x 8.5" (or 6" x 9")
- **Bleed:** Add 0.125" to all edges
- **Resolution:** 300 DPI minimum
- **Color Mode:** CMYK for print

### Estimated Spine Width
- 90,000 words ≈ 300-320 pages
- At 5.5x8.5: ~0.7" spine
- At 6x9: ~0.65" spine

## MIDJOURNEY WORKFLOW

1. **Generate base image** using primary prompt
2. **Create 4 variations**, select best
3. **Upscale** chosen image (2x or 4x)
4. **Download** and open in Photoshop/GIMP
5. **Add text layers** (title, subtitle, author)
6. **Export** as high-res PNG for KDP upload

### Typography Tips
- Use bold, readable fonts (Montserrat, Bebas Neue, Futura)
- Ensure contrast against background image
- Keep title large enough to read at thumbnail size (Amazon search results)
- Place author name at bottom, title at top or center

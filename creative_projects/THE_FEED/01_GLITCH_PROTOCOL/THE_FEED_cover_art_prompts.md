## DESIGN NOTES

### Color Progression (Series Cohesion)
- **Book 1:** Cool blues, teals, underground darkness (the discovery)
- **Book 2:** Purples, neon, digital aesthetic (the confrontation)
- **Book 3:** Warm ambers, golds, natural greens (the liberation)

### Typography Suggestions
- **Title Font:** Sans-serif, modern, slightly geometric (suggests technology)
- **Author Name:** Same family, lighter weight
- **Subtitle:** Smaller, possibly serif for contrast

### Visual Motifs to Maintain
- AR contact lens (appears in all three, evolving state)
- Tree/natural growth (Book 1 small, Book 3 dominant)
- Light source (artificial blue → neon purple → natural gold)

### Midjourney Tips
- Use `--no text, words, letters` to avoid gibberish text in images
- Add `--style raw` for less Midjourney aesthetic, more your vision
- Generate 4 variations, upscale the best, then use Photoshop for text overlay
- Consider hiring a cover designer for final typography (Midjourney for art only)

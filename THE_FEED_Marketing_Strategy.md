# THE FEED: Glitch Protocol — Marketing Strategy

## Overview
**Goal:** Maximize visibility, drive initial sales/reviews, and build momentum for long-term success.
**Key Tactic:** Free promotion campaign to generate downloads, reviews, and algorithmic boost.

---

## Phase 1: Foundation (Do This First)

### 1.1 Optimize Your Amazon Listing
- **Keywords:** Research and update backend keywords (use tools like Publisher Rocket or free alternatives)
- **Categories:** Ensure you're in the right browse categories (Sci-Fi > Cyberpunk, Thriller > Technological, etc.)
- **Description:** Compelling hook + bullet points + call-to-action. Front-load the hook!
- **A+ Content (if eligible):** Enhanced descriptions with images/formatting

### 1.2 Set Up Author Central
- Create/update Amazon Author Central profile
- Add author photo, bio, and link all your books
- This builds credibility and gives readers a place to follow you

### 1.3 Pricing Strategy
- **Regular Price:** $2.99-$4.99 for ebook (sweet spot for 70% royalty + perceived value)
- **Paperback:** Price at 2.5x print cost minimum
- **Free Promo:** Plan for 3-5 days (more on this below)

---

## Phase 2: KDP Select & Free Promotion

### 2.1 Enroll in KDP Select (if not already)
- 90-day exclusivity to Amazon
- Benefits: Kindle Unlimited (KU) reads count as revenue, promotional tools access
- **Free Book Promotions:** 5 days every 90 days (use strategically!)

### 2.2 Free Promotion Strategy
**Timing:**
- Run for 3-5 consecutive days (not scattered)
- Best days: Friday-Sunday or Thursday-Monday
- Avoid major holidays when people are distracted

**Pre-Promo Setup (1-2 weeks before):**
- [ ] Schedule promo dates in KDP dashboard
- [ ] Prepare all marketing materials (graphics, copy, emails)
- [ ] Notify your network (friends, family, email list)
- [ ] Submit to free book promotion sites (see list below)

**During Promo:**
- [ ] Post on all social channels (multiple times)
- [ ] Email your list
- [ ] Engage with anyone who shares/comments
- [ ] Monitor rankings and adjust if needed

**Post-Promo:**
- [ ] Thank everyone who helped
- [ ] Analyze results (downloads, KU reads, reviews generated)
- [ ] Return to regular price ($2.99-$4.99)
- [ ] Follow up with readers for reviews

### 2.3 Free Book Promotion Sites
Submit your free promo to these sites (many are free, some paid):

**Free Submission:**
- Freebooksy (sci-fi category)
- BookBub (free tier, hard to get)
- Robin Reads
- Ereader News Today
- BookDoggy
- Awesome Gang
- Indie Book of the Day
- Pretty-Hot

**Paid (but effective):**
- BookBub Featured Deals (gold standard, expensive but worth it)
- Freebooksy (paid tier = better placement)
- Robin Reads ($$)
- Ereader News Today ($$)

**Reddit Communities:**
- r/FreeEBOOKS
- r/KindleFreebies
- r/scifi
- r/cyberpunk

## Phase 3: Social Media Strategy

### 3.1 Platform Priorities

**Twitter/X (High Priority for Sci-Fi):**
- Post 2-3x daily during promo
- Use relevant hashtags: #SciFi #Cyberpunk #FreeBook #Kindle #IndieAuthor #BookTwitter
- Engage with sci-fi community (reply to authors, readers, fans)
- Share snippets, quotes, behind-the-scenes
- Pin the promo tweet

**Instagram:**
- Visual platform — create quote graphics, cover reveals
- Stories for countdown to promo
- Reels: "POV: You just found your next favorite cyberpunk novel"
- Hashtags: #Bookstagram #SciFiBooks #Cyberpunk #IndieAuthor

**TikTok (BookTok):**
- HUGE for book discovery right now
- Short videos: "Books that will make you question reality" type content
- Trending sounds + book recommendations
- Behind-the-scenes of writing

**Facebook:**
- Author page posts
- Relevant groups (Sci-Fi Book Promotions, Indie Authors, etc.)
- Facebook Ads (if budget allows)

**LinkedIn:**
- "I published a book" announcement
- Professional network may surprise you with support

### 3.2 Content Calendar (Free Promo Week)

**1 Week Before:**
- Teaser: "Something big is coming..."
- Behind-the-scenes: Writing process, inspiration
- Cover reveal with release date

**3 Days Before:**
- Countdown begins
- Share blurbs/excerpts
- "Mark your calendars"

**Day 1 of Promo:**
- Launch announcement (ALL platforms)
- "FREE for limited time!"
- Direct link to Amazon
- Ask for shares

**Day 2-3:**
- Quote graphics from the book
- Reader testimonials (if any)
- Reminder posts
- Thank you to sharers

**Final Day:**
- "Last chance!"
- Final push with urgency
- Thank everyone

**Post-Promo:**
- Results share ("We hit #1 in Cyberpunk!")
- Return to regular price announcement
- Request reviews

## Phase 4: Email Marketing

### 4.1 Build Your List
- Add signup link to your author bio
- Offer a free short story or bonus chapter for signups
- Use Mailchimp, ConvertKit, or Substack (free tiers available)

### 4.2 Email Sequence

**Pre-Promo:**
Subject: "THE FEED goes free in 3 days!"
- Personal note about the book
- Why you're running the promo
- Ask them to download and share

**Day 1 of Promo:**
Subject: "It's LIVE — download your free copy now"
- Direct link
- Request for reviews
- Social share buttons

**Final Day:**
Subject: "Last hours — get it before it's $4.99 again"
- Urgency
- Thank you

**Post-Promo:**
Subject: "Thank you + a request"
- Results/gratitude
- Direct ask for Amazon review (crucial!)

## Phase 5: Reviews & Long-Term

### 5.1 Getting Reviews (Critical!)
- **ARC Team:** Build a team of 10-20 readers who get free copies early in exchange for honest reviews
- **Newsletter Swaps:** Partner with other sci-fi authors to promote each other
- **Direct Ask:** Email list, social media — "If you read it, please review it"
- **Book Reviewers:** Reach out to book bloggers, BookTubers, BookTokers

### 5.2 Ongoing Marketing
- **Newsletter:** Monthly updates, new releases, behind-the-scenes
- **Sequel Setup:** Tease next book, build anticipation
- **Cross-Promotion:** Collaborate with other indie authors
- **Ads:** Amazon Ads, Facebook Ads (start small, $5-10/day, scale what works)

---

## Phase 6: Advanced Tactics

### 6.1 Amazon Ads
- **Sponsored Products:** Target similar authors/books
- **Lockscreen Ads:** Cheaper, good for visibility
- Start with $5-10/day, track ACOS (Advertising Cost of Sale)
- Pause ads that don't convert, scale winners

### 6.2 BookBub Featured Deal
- The holy grail of book promotion
- Apply even if you think you'll be rejected
- If accepted, expect hundreds/thousands of downloads
- Usually costs $100-500+ depending on category

### 6.3 Newsletter Swaps
- Find authors in your genre with similar-sized lists
- Promote their free book to your list, they promote yours
- Great way to reach new readers

### 6.4 Book Bundles
- Partner with other authors for themed bundles
- "Cyberpunk Starter Pack" type deals
- Everyone promotes to their audience

## Final Notes

**The free promo is about more than free downloads — it's about:**
1. Algorithm juice (Amazon's "also bought" recommendations)
2. Reviews (social proof for future buyers)
3. Visibility (getting on genre bestseller lists)
4. Email list growth
5. Momentum

**Don't be discouraged if:**
- First promo doesn't hit huge numbers
- Reviews come in slowly
- Sales drop after promo

This is a long game. Each book builds on the last. THE FEED is your foundation.
